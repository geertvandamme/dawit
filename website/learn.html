Learn with DawIt

Less guessing.
More “oh, that’s why.”

Read a little. Play an example. Change something in your song. DawIt keeps the explanation close to the thing you’re trying to understand.

Three ways the app helps

From a question
to an “I hear it.”

  1. Learn the idea.

    Open a theory guide from the tool you’re using, or browse everything in View → Theory Guides… (Cmd+G).

  2. Try it in context.

    Play the examples and change the controls. Many guides have a “Your song”, “Your track” or “Your clip” topic that reads your own work.

  3. See and hear the result.

    Live pictures follow the playhead or settings. Explanations, meters and starting points help you decide what to try next.
